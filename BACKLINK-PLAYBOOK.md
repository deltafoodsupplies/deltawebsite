# Backlink & Citation Playbook — Delta Food Supplies

Links from relevant sites are one of the strongest ranking signals you control.
Work through this list top to bottom; each item is a single email or form.

## 1. Vendor "where to buy" listings (highest value — do these first)

Email your sales rep at each brand and ask to be added as a listed distributor
for TX / NM / OK / AR / LA. Copy-paste template at the bottom.

| Brand | Where to ask |
|---|---|
| Nanak Foods | nanakfoods.com — has a "Locations" page; ask your rep to list Delta as a regional distributor |
| Viswas | viswasfoods.in — ask for the US distributor listing |
| Himalayan Momos | himalayanmomo.com — has a "where to buy" section |
| Wagh Bakri | waghbakritea.com — US distributor network page |
| Veeba | veeba.in — foodservice partners |
| MDH | mdhspices.com — US importer/distributor listings |
| Tamicon / Pachranga / Ustad Banne Nawab | Ask reps directly — smaller brands often add distributor links same-week |

## Tips

- Pace it: a handful of listings a week looks natural; 50 in one day doesn't.
- Always link to https://deltafoodsupplies.com (with https, no trailing page).
- Once the guides section grows, pitch a guide (e.g., the Diwali stocking guide)
  to community newsletters — content earns links a homepage can't.
