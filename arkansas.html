Arkansas Indian and South Asian food distributor

Wholesale Indian and South Asian food supply for Arkansas retailers and kitchens.

Delta Food Supplies supports Arkansas grocery stores, restaurants, caterers, temples, and foodservice teams with Indian and South Asian groceries, frozen products, dairy, sweets, snacks, and restaurant supply items.

Serving Little Rock, Fayetteville, Bentonville, Rogers, and surrounding Arkansas markets.

Arkansas connects to our Dallas warehouse along the I-30 corridor through Texarkana to Little Rock, with Northwest Arkansas — Bentonville, Rogers, Springdale, and Fayetteville — forming a second focus area. The corporate hub around Bentonville has drawn a fast-growing South Asian professional community, and grocery demand there has grown with it.

We help Arkansas grocers and restaurants stock everyday staples, frozen foods, dairy, sweets, and snack brands their customers recognize, with order planning that suits smaller-market volumes. Our team can help you plan product lists, brand availability, and wholesale supply.

  • Wholesale Indian and South Asian grocery, spice, rice, atta, dal, chutney, and pantry staples
  • Frozen appetizers, parathas, ready-to-heat items, dairy, sweets, snacks, and beverages
  • Product sourcing for flavors connected to India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Afghanistan, and nearby South Asian cuisines
  • Dallas HQ pickup for eligible orders plus shipping and distribution support
